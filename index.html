FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Every job is different, so every quote is free and based on your property: the size, the number of stories, and the services you need. Send a quote request or text us a few photos and we'll get you an exact price.

Do you clean second-story and hard-to-reach windows?

Yes. We use water-fed poles and professional equipment to safely clean high windows, second stories, and commercial glass.

Are you insured?

Yes. JB Exterior Cleaning is an LLC and carries insurance on every job.

Do I need to be home?

Not for exterior work. For interior window cleaning, someone will need to let us in. We'll confirm the details when we schedule.

How often should windows and gutters be cleaned?

Most homes do best with window cleaning and gutter cleaning once or twice a year. Spring and fall are the most popular times. Businesses with storefront glass often want it more often.

Is soft washing safe for my siding and roof?

Yes. Soft washing uses low pressure and the right cleaning solution to remove mildew, algae, and grime without damaging siding, brick, or shingles.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Sallisaw, Oklahoma and serve homes and businesses within about a 90-minute drive, across Eastern Oklahoma and Western Arkansas.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. We clean storefronts, offices, and large commercial buildings, and we're a registered Cherokee Nation vendor.
